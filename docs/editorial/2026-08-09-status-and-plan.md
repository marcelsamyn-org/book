## How to talk about the book (the spoken pitch)

The Mindvalley formulation is the book's spine sentence, found by having to say it out loud: *meaning is when we choose imperfection — and AI, whose whole existence stems from error minimization, structurally cannot.* It's the Conclusion compressed ("decides to *not* go to that optimum… leave the imperfection as it is"), and the partner example is already in the manuscript ("The love we give feels special when it's given *despite* clear reasons why not to").

Two-beat shape to keep: **diagnosis** (AI is an error-minimization machine — a pull toward the expected, and nothing expected ever meant anything to anyone) → **answer with the partner punchline** (finding the perfect partner is convenience; *choosing* an imperfect one is love).

One caution: don't let "choosing imperfection" swallow the book's other meaning-source — the struggle you *didn't* choose (the tumor, the not-knowing, the God passage). The Conclusion unifies them as "risky randomness," and the partner example is secretly the synthesis: you choose the imperfect partner, *and then* they surprise and hurt you in ways you can't control. The pitch should end there, not at the choosing.
