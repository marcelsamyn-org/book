## Approved product description

Artificial intelligence is getting better at the work we once thought only humans could do. What happens to meaning when effort becomes optional?

In Sacred Struggle, Marcel Samyn starts with how AI actually works, then follows its effects into learning, work, love, and creativity. Drawing on research and personal experience, he asks what makes a life satisfying when machines can solve more and more of our problems.

The book explores the difference between comfort and fulfillment, the value of chosen difficulty, and the decisions we still need to make for ourselves. It offers a way to think about AI’s place in your life, and about what you want to keep doing for yourself.
